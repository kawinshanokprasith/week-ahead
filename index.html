Tips
  • Loose tasks have no day yet. They wait in the tray until you place them.
  • Drag a card onto a day to plan it, or back to the tray.
  • Right-click a card for Delete or Cancel.
  • X or Space marks the focused card done. A adds a task.
  • The view menu (Week) changes how many days show.
  • The refresh button pulls the latest meetings from Outlook.
